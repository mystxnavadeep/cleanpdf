# AP Intermediate 2nd Year - Clean Digital Textbook Reconstruction 📚

This repository contains **digitally reconstructed, deskewed, pure-white (#FFFFFF) background, and 300 DPI searchable OCR text-layered digital textbooks** for Andhra Pradesh Intermediate 2nd Year (Baby Bullet Series: Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry).

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Engineering Features
* **Pure Digital White Background (`#FFFFFF`)**: Complete elimination of photographic artifacts, paper fiber texture, bedsheets, uneven lighting, and camera shadows.
* **Original Color Integrity**: Red headings (`{8 Marks}`, `(1) DETERMINANTS`), blue annotations, red asterisks, and circuit labels preserved in 100% full-chroma print colors.
* **Mathematical & Formula Precision**: Every single formula, fraction, radical, matrix, integral, and derivative preserved with sharp, anti-aliased digital ink.
* **100% Searchable OCR Layer**: Full OCR text layer embedded across all 354 pages (100% selectable and copyable).
* **Standardized ISO A4 Canvas**: Uniform portrait layout (595.2 × 841.9 pt) with balanced digital margins.
* **Double-Page Spread Splitting**: All 113 double-page photographed spreads split into individual reading pages.
* **Page Orientation**: Maths 8 Marks Page 50 rotated 90° clockwise into upright portrait reading view.
