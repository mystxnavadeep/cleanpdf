# AP Intermediate 2nd Year - Clean Digital Textbook Reconstruction & Bilingual Study Editions 📚

This repository contains **digitally reconstructed, deskewed, pure-white (#FFFFFF) background, 300 DPI searchable OCR text-layered digital textbooks**, along with **Facing-Page Interleaved Bilingual Study Editions (English + Tenglish)** for Andhra Pradesh Intermediate 2nd Year (Baby Bullet Series: Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry).

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Engineering Features
* **Interleaved Facing-Page Bilingual Architecture**:
  * **Odd Pages (1, 3, 5, ...)**: Clean digital reconstructed original textbook page (English) with original diagrams, equations, and full color integrity.
  * **Even Pages (2, 4, 6, ...)**: Specially created companion Tenglish study page matching that exact English page (explaining what the question asks, given values, formulas, step-by-step calculations, and exam marks tips in natural Telugu-English).
  * **Total Pages**: 354 original English + 354 created Tenglish = **708 pages** across all 10 PDFs.
* **Pure Digital White Background (`#FFFFFF`)**: Complete elimination of photographic artifacts, paper fiber texture, bedsheets, uneven lighting, and camera shadows.
* **Original Color Integrity**: Red headings (`{8 Marks}`, `(1) DETERMINANTS`), blue annotations, red asterisks, and circuit labels preserved in 100% full-chroma print colors.
* **Mathematical & Formula Precision**: Every single formula, fraction, radical, matrix, integral, and derivative preserved with sharp, anti-aliased digital ink.
* **100% Searchable OCR Layer**: Full OCR text layer embedded across all 354 original pages (100% selectable and copyable).
* **Standardized ISO A4 Canvas**: Uniform portrait layout (595.2 × 841.9 pt) with balanced digital margins.
* **Double-Page Spread Splitting**: All 113 double-page photographed spreads split into individual reading pages.
