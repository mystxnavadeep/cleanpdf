# Telugu-English (Tenglish) Academic Translation Report

## 1. Methodology
Every question, answer, and mathematical proof is translated into natural, conversational, exam-oriented Tenglish.

## 2. Linguistic Rules Applied
1. **Technical Terminology Preserved**: Terms like 'Matrix Inversion Method', 'Determinant', 'Cofactors', 'Stationary Waves', 'Haber Process' retain their standard English names.
2. **Conversational Clarity**: Explanations answer 'Given values enti?', 'Find cheyyalsindi enti?', 'Ee formula enduku use chestunnam?', and 'Final answer ela vachindi?'.
3. **Formula Variables**: Every Greek letter and variable is explained in simple Telugu-English terms.
